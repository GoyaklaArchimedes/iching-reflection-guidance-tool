    The Eight Trigrams

    Heaven represents creative force. Earth reflects receptivity and support. Thunder symbolises awakening and movement. Water represents emotional depth and uncertainty. Mountain reflects stillness and boundaries. Wind symbolises gradual influence. Fire reflects illumination and clarity. Lake symbolises openness and connection.

    Changing Lines

    Changing lines indicate movement and transformation within the situation. They reveal where energy is shifting rather than remaining fixed.

    Primary and Relating Hexagrams

    The primary hexagram reflects the present symbolic atmosphere. The relating hexagram shows how the situation may be moving or transforming through the changing lines.
